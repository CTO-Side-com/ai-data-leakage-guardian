# Primary source register

Reviewed 2026-11-20. The 37 objective titles and test suggestions are original CTOside material. These links inform engineering review; they are not a compliance checklist or a claim that a particular vendor configuration is current.

| Source | Use |

Use current official service documentation and authorized configuration evidence for every provider-specific claim. Versioned OWASP links are explicitly identified; the pack does not assert they are the newest edition. No OWASP, NIST or vendor endorsement is claimed.
